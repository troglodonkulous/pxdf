Generative, tileable pattern engine — pixels are computed live from a periodic formula, not decoded from a video file.

On seamlessness: the live preview never actually "loops" — it re-evaluates the pattern from time on every frame, so there's no cut or re-buffer to see. For files, use Export PNG Sequence and your media server's gapless/frame-accurate loop mode — compressed video (.webm/.mp4) commonly stutters at the loop point in ordinary players.
